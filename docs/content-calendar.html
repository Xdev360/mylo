1 post a week · no CTA

Closed testing runs underneath this. You are not selling anything yet — you are building an audience that is already warm when the waitlist opens. One post, every Wednesday, same time. Consistency is the whole mechanic.

This phase

It's hard to do it alone.

Our first app was called Goalden. It worked, and people used it, and we still stopped — because every productivity app, ours included, is built for one person in a quiet room. You set the goal alone. You miss it alone. Nobody notices either way.

Mylo is the second attempt, built the other way round. It's a private room for you and the people you're actually doing life with — your partner, your crew, your one friend who'll notice. Your goals and theirs on one board. No feed. No strangers.

It's still fine if it's just you. It's just easier when it isn't.

Rules that do not move

  • Post at 7–9pm Lagos, except launch day — that one goes out at 8am so the day has room to run.
  • Phase 1 has no call to action. Not one. The discipline is the point: you are building an audience, not a queue you cannot serve.
  • Never put a naira figure on the reward pool. Ten percent of revenue, published monthly.
  • No post links into the app. The waitlist is the gate and testers get their link by email.
  • Real quotes only. Two slots need a tester quote. If you do not have one, skip the slot. Do not invent a testimonial.
  • Reply to every comment for the first hour. At this size the comments are the distribution.
  • The date moves if the product says so. If the Phase 1 exit condition is not met by 25 October, push 3 December. Launching wider into a product people leave after three days just burns the attention.
