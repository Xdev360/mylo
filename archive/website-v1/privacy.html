Privacy

Mylo · Wintech Studio · Lagos, Nigeria · last updated September 2026

What we collect on this page

Your email address, when you join the waitlist. Optionally the referral code you arrived with and the page that sent you. That's it — no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics, no ad scripts.

What we do with it

One email when your invite is ready. We don't sell it, rent it, or add it to a newsletter. If you refer a friend, we count the referral against your code; we never show your address to them or theirs to you.

Inside the app

Your account, goals, rooms, messages, stories and XP are stored so the app works. Rooms are private: only members can read a room, enforced at the database, not just in the interface. Stories expire after 24 hours. Connections you link (Google Calendar, Notion, Telegram) can be unlinked in one tap from You → Connect, which removes our access.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase and served through Vercel. Payments, when they launch, are handled by Paystack — we never see or store card numbers.

Your choices

  • Leave the waitlist or delete your account and data: email hello@staymylo.com from the address you signed up with.
  • Ask what we hold about you, and we'll send it.
