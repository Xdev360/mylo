Today

One screen. What you said you'd do, in the order the day happens.

Morning, Afternoon, Night, Anytime. Each block carries its own counter so you know what's left before you scroll. Finish something and it peels underneath into Completed.

The day view

Progress you can see. Yours, and theirs.

The bar at the top shows everyone in the room. Your goals stay on your board; a Shared goal shows on everyone's. Hold and swipe a card down to mark it done — and Mylo reacts.

  • Ten auto-categories with their own colour: Fitness, Study, Prayer, Health, Food, Work, Creative, Chores, Social, Finance

FAQ

The short answers.

Is Mylo built, or is this just a landing page?
Built and running. Today, Goals, Rooms, Chat, Calendar, Apps, Mylo AI, Look, the wallet and the in-chat games are all live. The waitlist is about pacing who gets in so early rooms stay small — not about whether the app exists.
What happens after I leave my email?
You get one private email with your link to join, when your batch opens. Oldest signups first. No drip sequence, no newsletter, and your address is never shown to anyone else.
Do I need an app store?
No. Mylo runs in the browser and installs to your home screen — icon, splash screen, push notifications. Same account on phone and desktop, and the desktop layout is its own thing, not a stretched phone.
Can I use it on my own?
Yes — "Just me" is one of the setups Mylo asks about during onboarding, and Mylo AI fills the accountability gap. Mylo is built for doing it together, but solo is a real mode, not a downgrade. You can add someone to a room later without starting over.
Who can see my goals?
Only people in your room. Nothing is public, and rooms are joined by request — an owner or admin has to allow you in. Inside the room, your personal goals never land on anyone else's Today board, but they are visible under Goals → Everyone and on the calendar. Only goals you mark Shared can be edited or ticked off by someone else.
What's the difference between $MYL and credits?
$MYL is earned inside the app — finishing systems, winning games, completing goals — and spent on unlocking apps like Colour Rush (200) and Scrabble (350). AI credits are bought with real money and spent one per Mylo AI call. Free plans get a monthly credit allowance; packs start at ₦2,990 for 100.
How does the reward pool work?
Season 1 is counting now: your XP from day one counts toward the first round. Every month 10% of Mylo's revenue goes into a published pool, and your rank on verified XP — systems finished, goals completed, games won against real people, streaks, time in the app — decides your share. The top 10 each week are named on our socials and get a month of Pro. Cash payouts come from the pool at the end of each round, from revenue, never from promises. Bot wins don't count and farmed accounts are removed.
Is my data safe?
Rooms are private by design and every table is locked down with row-level security — the browser key can only ever see what your account is allowed to. Integrations can be unlinked in one tap from You → Connect.
